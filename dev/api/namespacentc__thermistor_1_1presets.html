The 39 nominal rows of the TDK NTCG163JF103FT1S, -40 to +150 degC in 5 degC steps.

Source: a secondary listing of the nominal column of the vendor's resistance-temperature table. The datasheet (TDK Rev.3, 2023-05-08, sha256 in docs/datasheets.yml) is one page and prints no table: it links a separate 1 degC step file that could not be fetched. The rows are not verified row by row; they are consistent with the sheet's B values (B25/85 3435 K, B25/50 3380 K typ.) and range (-40 to +150 degC), checked by a test.

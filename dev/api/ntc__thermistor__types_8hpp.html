Detailed Description

Public types of the NTC thermistor driver: errors, Result<T>, the curve and the configuration, the reading, and Validate().

Copyright
Copyright (c) 2026 The driver-ntc-thermistor authors. SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT

The driver knows no part. A thermistor is described by a Curve (a table of resistance and temperature rows, or R25 and B for the beta equation) and a divider by the rest of Config. Every quantity is an integer: resistance in ohms, temperature in milli-degrees Celsius, voltage in millivolts, tolerance in parts per million.
